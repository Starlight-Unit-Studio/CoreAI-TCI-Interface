# CoreAI: TCI Interface Free Community Policy

CoreAI: TCI Interface (Quantum software family) is distributed by Starlight Unit Studios as a free source-available community project.

For the official CoreAI: TCI Interface project, the current policy is:

- no purchase price
- no paid license tier
- no subscription fee
- no paid white-label program
- no donation mechanism tied to CoreAI: TCI Interface
- no advertising or sponsorship monetization inside CoreAI: TCI Interface
- no paid official support package
- no license phone-home, installation reporting, or user-count telemetry

Internal use by individuals, communities, businesses, educational institutions, and other organizations remains free when the E.M.B.E.R. CoreUI Community Source License and required branding are respected.

Branding exceptions are not automatic. If Starlight Unit Studios grants an individual written exception under the current policy, it is handled without an CoreAI: TCI Interface license fee. The project is not obligated to approve an exception.

This policy describes the official project's present non-monetized operation. It is not a promise concerning unrelated Starlight Unit Studios works and does not replace `LICENSE.de.md` or `LICENSE.md`. Any future policy change would apply prospectively, be documented transparently, and would not withdraw rights already granted for earlier copies.
